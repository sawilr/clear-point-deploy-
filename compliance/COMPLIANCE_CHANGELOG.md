## 2026-09-14 — Round-5 adversarial re-verification and remediation (branch fix/master-audit-2026-09-10)
- **trigger:** the round-5 red team, run against the round-4 fixes and against four control surfaces at once. 52 documented attacks, 59 reproduced defects: 2 P0, 15 P1, 20 P2, 15 P3, 7 P4. Every finding carries the literal input and output observed and a command to re-run it.
- **the headline finding is about the process, not the code:** five of the six P1 defects in the reply filter and all five in the Medicare figure backstop were **regressions of round-4 fixes**. Behaviour that was correct at 419c5cd had been broken by a fix written against one adversarial string and never measured against the traffic it had to keep working for. Two of them were P0.
- **P0 — emergency post-condition (42 CFR 422.2274(g) adjacent; life safety).** Round 4 asked whether an instruction verb appeared anywhere within 40 characters of a 911-shaped token. "Call", "dial", "contact", "llame" and "marcar" are ordinary Medicare-service vocabulary, so seven plain sentences satisfied the invariant. A caller who wrote "I am having chest pain and I cannot breathe" could be answered with "Please call your plan at 9-1-1-ext 4 for that billing question. What is your ZIP code?" and the net stayed silent. The test is now adjacency, not co-occurrence: the number must be the direct object of an instruction verb through a closed connector set, or carry an urgency marker, or be named as the emergency number. Reference numbering and unit or time tails disqualify it. Default-deny by design — see KNOWN-LIMITATIONS #22.
- **P0 — date-of-birth redaction (HIPAA Safe Harbor, 45 CFR 164.514(b)(2)(i)(C)).** The entire date block, including the generic plausible-birth-year sweep, sat behind a test on the supplied `date_of_birth`. Only Smart Medicare Review supplies one; Zara sends the empty string and LeadForm, the support bot and Clara send nothing. On four surfaces out of five the redaction was dead code and a birth date typed into the caller's own story reached the enrichment model verbatim. The generic sweep now always runs; spelled-out and year-less birth dates are caught beside a birth cue; an ISO date carrying a time no longer escapes its own trailing guard.
- **P1 — TCPA.** The consent classifier refused both answers to its own question: 21 of 28 affirmative phrasings were re-prompted while plain "no" was accepted, and "stop" — the statutory SMS opt-out keyword this site's own pages teach — classified as unclear. Refusal is now broad and checked first; agreement stays strict.
- **P1 — CMS TPMO 42 CFR 422.2267(e)(41).** Zara's opening block carried no (e)(41) sentence; the round-4 fix had landed only in the "who are you" education reply, so a visitor who opened the chat and stayed in it never saw it. It now renders with the privacy note.
- **P1 — call recording 42 CFR 422.2274(g)(2)(ii) and FTC material connection.** On `/support` and `/es/support`, the only two routes with no footer, that disclosure is the sole carrier of both duties — and it was clipped by up to 86px at load and vanished entirely once the notice collapsed after the first turn. A disclosure cut mid-word was never made. It is now a persistent strip outside the scrolling transcript, measured fully visible in 12 of 12 route and width combinations.
- **P1 — FTC net impression.** The "$0 premium" qualifier had been applied to the homepage and not to the Medicare Advantage page, which is the page the fixed homepage card links to.
- **P1 — accessibility (WCAG 2.4.3, 2.4.7, 4.1.2).** The guide dialog's focus trap held a keyboard user inside a box they could not scroll while the page behind ran away; the Zara launcher stayed in the tab order with zero opacity while the cookie banner was open, and Enter there opened the chat over the consent decision.
- **P1 — privacy.** Two lead fields reached the enrichment model raw, above the notes delimiter: a state of "Maria Gonzalez, DOB 03/15/1950, SSN 123-45-6789, 917-555-0123" went through verbatim, and a coverage value carrying an injected instruction landed on its own line. Both are allow-listed now, and `api/_lib/lead-intel.js` caps and single-lines every metadata value at its own boundary.
- **new build gates:** `scripts/check-allowlist-drift.mjs` fails the build when a producer can send a value the server allow-list discards — on its first run it found a third live instance of that bug, `lead_source: 'zara_education'`, which had been tagging every Zara education lead `Source-other`. `scripts/check-zero-cost-claims.mjs` fails when a zero-premium claim has no cost qualifier in its own sentence or the next. `scripts/check-figures-year.mjs` was hardened twice: a decimal tail ("$283.50") used to pass the value check, and a stale comment naming another year used to shadow the real constant. All three are proven against copies of the tree containing the defect.
